scRareBench

__TITLE__

Standalone benchmark explorer for integration quality, scIB-compatible scores, rare-population preservation, clustering outcomes, and failure patterns. Canonical benchmark scores remain immutable; sensitivity and what-if controls are explicitly exploratory and never overwrite reported results.

Interpretation note. Failure-archetype assignments use provisional scRareBench thresholds. Treat them as diagnostic annotations; threshold sensitivity should be assessed before manuscript-level conclusions.
